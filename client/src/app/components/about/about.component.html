Up and running in three steps

From your first idea to your first finished task.

  1. 01

    Make it yours

    Create an account, follow the link in your verification email, then sign in to your own private board.

  2. 02

    Capture a task

    Choose Add task, write your task, and hit Save. Your new card appears at the top of To Do.

  3. 03

    Keep things moving

    Drag cards between columns as work progresses, or reorder them within a column to set priorities.

Working with tasks

Small actions that keep your board in shape.

  • Add a little detail

    Task fields expand as you type, with room for multiple lines and notes.

  • Edit in place

    Use the pencil on a card, then Save. Choose Cancel or press Esc to discard changes.

  • Move at your pace

    Drag to change a task’s stage or its position in a column.

  • Clear what you no longer need

    Use the trash icon on a card, then confirm to delete it.

Break it into smaller steps

Add - [ ] item (or - [] item) for a sub-task, and - [x] item for a completed one. Click a checkbox right on the card to toggle it.

Built around your day

A few useful things to know.

  • Light or dark

    Switch themes from the header. Your preference is saved across sessions.

  • At home on mobile

    Open the menu for navigation, your email, and sign out on smaller screens.

  • Your account, your control

    Change your password or permanently close your account and delete its tasks from Account. Recover access with Forgot password? on the sign-in page.

  • A board of your own

    Each account sees only its own tasks. Sign out from the header or mobile menu when you’re finished.
